The three gaps I would fix first

  1. Publish /llms.txt. A short markdown index of the pages you want assistants to read and quote. Cheap, static, no dependency.
  2. Add a real sitemap and crawler rules. Generate the XML sitemap, declare it with a Sitemap: line in robots.txt, and add explicit User-agent rules for the AI crawlers you want to allow.
  3. Add the missing template tags. Organization plus SoftwareApplication JSON-LD, a rel="canonical", and og:url — three small edits that apply site-wide from one template.
